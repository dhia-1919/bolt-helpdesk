What Boolanga does for Bolt

Boolanga runs the logistics behind merchant onboarding. When a new restaurant or store joins Bolt, Boolanga makes sure the right devices and onboarding items reach them, and takes them back when they leave.

  • Stores your stock in local warehouses: Sunmi V3 tablets, SIM cards, tabletops and stickers.
  • Ships to merchants from the orders you place in OMS, and tracks every parcel to delivery.
  • Activates devices in the markets where activation is still needed.
  • Handles exchanges and returns, including refurbishing used devices so they can be sent out again.
  • Runs OMS, where you place orders and see stock and delivery status.
  • Supports you through Customer Support tickets and the shared Slack channels.
